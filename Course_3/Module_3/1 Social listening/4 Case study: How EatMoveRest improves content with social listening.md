## Case study: How EatMoveRest improves content with social listening

### The company background
Eatmove rest was established to coach people to achieve healthy and sustainable lifestyles.
- Vegan meal planning
- Plant-based recipes
- Expert advice
- Coaching
- Practical tips for sustainable living

### The challenges

- Large volume of user comments
- No barriers or limits on expression in feedback
- Inability to reply to or address every comment

#### Large volume of user comments
YouTube and instagram comments are endless

#### No barriers or limits on expression in feedback
It can be hard to identify useful feedback from thousands of people digitally "shouting" in strong language or bold text.

#### Inability to reply to or address every comment
Addressing the input from a large number of followers can be a difficult task to navigate.


### The approach
- Identify which channel is a priority to review comments
- Offer followers designated times to provide feedback via chosen methods

#### Prioritized reviews
EatMoveRest started providing the comments on it's youtube channel

#### Designated times and methods for feedback
Setting deignated times and methods for feedback enables a more detailed focus on constructive comments. Noisy and sometimes tasteless comments can be ignored

Q&A feature in the instagram
The feedback they get on their products, apps, website, recipes and content is in one organized location, and they can reply to users promptly.


### The results

#### Revrsing changes to content
Listening to user's feedback on background music and updated accordingly

#### Improving the meal planning app
Q&A features enabled it
Because of it savvy and dedicated social listening practices, EatMoveRest heard its users and started to updated the receipes in the app more frequently

### Conclusion
A business needs to actively listen on social media to understand how users are interacting with its brand.
Feedback will be positive and constructive, negative and biased, or sometimes contentious.
Even a brand like EatMoveRest with a loyal base of followers can benefit from makeing changes to its business practices or content after receiving constructive feedback through social listening.
