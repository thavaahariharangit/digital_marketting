## Social Listening

1. Social listening is the process of tracking and analyzing conversations and trends related to a company's brand. Social listening can help a company identify problems customers are having with a brand. The company can then offer a solution to these problems on social media platforms.

2. Keywords
- Industry buzzwords are an example of keywords that can help a company find conversations about their brand.
- Hashtags related to the brand are an example of keyword that can help a company find conversations about their brands
- Brand and productnames are an example of keywords that can help a company find conversations about their brand.

3. Actions
- The insights gained from social media can help a company create content their followers are interested in.
- The insights gained from social media can help a company adjust their marketing strategy to fit the current marketplace.
- The insights gained from social media can help a company come up with new ideas based on trends in their industry.

4. Tools
- Hubspot, Sprout Social and Hootsuite are all social listening tools a company can use to track and analyze coversations and trends related to their brand.
