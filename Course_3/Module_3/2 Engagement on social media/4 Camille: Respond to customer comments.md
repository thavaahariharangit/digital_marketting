## Camille: Respond to social media comments

- Social media marketing and social media listening are two very different things
- By approaching the user with empathy, you're able to show that you're willing to help them, that you care about them, and that they're not just another number.
- When acknowledging that a customer has a positive experience with your brand, thanking them for their continued business, and complementing them, you build connection and foster community amongst those customers.
- If I could give myself advice for when I was first starting out in social media marketing, I would tell myself to have grace and to not take things personally.
- You never know what the user is going through on the other end of the screen
