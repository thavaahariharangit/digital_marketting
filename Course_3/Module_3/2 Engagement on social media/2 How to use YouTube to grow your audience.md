## How to use YouTube to grow your audience

YouTube is an incredible place to connect with customers all over the world who are coming to YouTube to be educated, entertained, and informed about their passions.

There's really no requirements for what content is successful. The only this that matters is that it is authentic.

## Digital marketers looking to grow their audience on YouTube
- Spend time on the platform
- Become a fan
- Subscribe to creators who interest you
- Identify key stories you want to tell
- Identify themes that are important to your business
