## Social media engagement

### Social media engagement 
Measures how people are interacting with your social media accounts and content

Note: 
Social media engagement measures how people are interacting with a company's social media accounts and content. Studying engagement can help a company determine whether their content resonates with an audience.

- Respond to comments and mentions
- Provide excellent customer service
- Respond to customers quickly
- Develop an authentic, personal voice
- Know your audience
- Create valuable content
- Get creative
    - Contests
    - Polls
    - Quizzes
    - Ask questions
    - Spotlight customers and employees
    - Elicit user-generated content
