## Increase your followers on Twitter

### Follower
Someone who opts in to receive updates from a business or brand on a social media platform

### Tweet
Any message posted to Twitter and may contain elements like text, photos, videos, links and audio

### To increase your followers on Twitter
- Engage with your audience often
- Tweet frequently
- Use hashtag
- Include visuals
- Write a creative bio
- Promote your social media accounts through other channels


### Hashtag
A word or phrase proceded by the pound symbol that indicates that a piece of content relates to a specific topic or catgory.
