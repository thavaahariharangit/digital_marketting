## Respond to social media users

### Why should you respond?

- way to get customers to engage
- you make them feel heard
- Accessible to people who read the replies
- indicates that your brand cares about the customer experience
- What ever the platform you are in, engaging with your customer base is usually a good idea.

### How should you respond?
- **If you are responding to positive feedback:** 
    - acknowledge and thank them
    - consider reposting
    - ensure many people as possible see that feedback
- **If you are responding to questions or general feedback:**
    - reply briefly with clear enthusiasm
    - should be fun and informative for both parties
    - your respond should indicate that you are having fun
    - creative language and exclamation marks
- **If you are responding to negative comments:**
    - remain calm and use helpful, polite and approachable language
    - offer rfunds and promotional codes, if something really bad
    - Ask them to send you a direct message so the issue can be resolved quickly
- **If you are responding to internet trolls:**
    - don't do it.
    - An internet troll is a person who intentionally antogonizes others online by posting inflammatory, unneccessary, or offensive comments or other disruptive content that shouldn't necessrily be replied.
    - Delete the comment or hiding the replies.

### Key takeaways

- When it comes to interacting with users on social media, each reply requires careful thought and consideration
- Whether the comments are positive, general feedback, or negative, you'll want to be strategic about when, how and where your reply.
- Feel free to keep these tips in mind as you move forward on your digital marketing journey and leverage the help of any team members who may have experience replying to users.
